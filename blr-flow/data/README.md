# Dataset provenance

17,280 synthetic hourly observations over 120 days at six named junctions. Illustrative offsets and rush-hour curves are assumptions, not observations.

Generated/assembled by the included training script, not scraped from real applicants, employers or traffic sources. Generated data/corpus is included for reproduction. No claim of measured real-world performance.

Synthetic data and generator code: MIT. Seed: 42 where randomness is used.
